Your first iPod.
Your music.
On Your desktop.

KliqPlayer is a click-wheel music player that floats on your desktop. Access your Spotify library or use your own local music.

It feels like the real one.

Not a skin. Every wheel, button and screen is redrawn to behave like the device you remember.

A click wheel…

Scroll with your trackpad or mouse and hear the tick under your thumb. Try this one.

A drawer…

Pull the drawer, pick another one. It unplugs, swaps, and plugs back into the earbud cable.

A Spotify account or your own music…

Your own folder (MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV, playlists), or your Spotify Premium library.
